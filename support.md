# East Europe Radio Relay - Support

East Europe Radio Relay plays live radio stations from Eastern Europe on your iPhone. Pick a station and press play.

## Report a problem

Tap the **Report** button in the app. It sends us the technical log so we can see what went wrong. You can also write to [mic1230@gmail.com](mailto:mic1230@gmail.com) and tell us what happened and which station you were playing.

## Questions and answers

**The station stops playing.** Check that your phone is online (Wi-Fi or mobile data), then press play again. If it keeps stopping, tap **Report** so we can look at it.

**There is no sound on the lock screen.** Make sure the phone is not on silent and the volume is up, then press play on the lock screen controls. If it is still silent, open the app, start the station again and tap **Report**.

**How do I change the language?** Tap the gear button in the app, then Language, and pick System, English, Română or Українська. It changes at once.

**How do I turn alerts off?** In the app open Settings, then Alerts, and switch them off. You can also turn them off in iOS Settings under Notifications.
