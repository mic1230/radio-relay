# East Europe Radio Relay - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10/29/2026

East Europe Radio Relay is made by Mircea Novac. Contact: [mic1230@gmail.com](mailto:mic1230@gmail.com).

## What the app collects

- **A technical log.** App events such as which station played, when playback stopped and why, and connection errors.
- **Listening sessions.** Which station you listened to, and when the session started and ended.
- **A random device label.** The app makes it by itself. It is not your name, your phone's name or any number tied to you.
- **App version, iOS version and language.**

## Why

To fix problems and improve the app, and to see which stations people listen to.

## Where it goes

To the developer's own server. It is not linked to a person. There is no account, and the app does not ask for your location, name, email, contacts or photos.

## What we do not do

No advertising. No tracking across other apps or websites. We never sell or share this information.

## How long we keep it

Up to 12 months, then it is deleted.

## Your choices

- The app has no off switch for the technical log.
- You can stop all collection by deleting the app.
- You can email us to ask for deletion of the data that belongs to your device label. Tell us which device label is yours and we will delete it.

## Push notifications

Only if you allow them. If you do, your phone's push token is stored on the developer's server so we can send you alerts. You can switch them off in the app under Settings, then Alerts, or in the iOS Settings app.

## Third-party streams

Stations are played from their broadcasters' public stream links. Those broadcasters may see your IP address, like any website you visit.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
